## Frequently asked questions
Q: What is the difference between an AI agent and a chatbot?
A: An AI agent is a more general term that refers to any autonomous system that uses AI to perform tasks, while a chatbot is a specific type of AI agent that is designed to interact with humans through text or voice conversations.
Q: How much does it cost to develop an AI agent?
A: The cost of developing an AI agent can vary widely, depending on the complexity of the project and the technology used. However, with the use of cloud-based services and open-source tools, it is possible to develop an AI agent at a relatively low cost.

A: Yes, AI agents can be used for customer support, and are increasingly being used by companies to provide 24/7 support to their customers. AI-powered chatbots can help answer common customer inquiries, freeing up human customer support agents to focus on more complex issues.
Q: What are the potential risks of using AI agents?
A: The potential risks of using AI agents include data privacy concerns, bias in decision-making, and the potential for AI agents to be used for malicious purposes. However, these risks can be mitigated through the use of robust security measures and careful design and testing of AI agents.
