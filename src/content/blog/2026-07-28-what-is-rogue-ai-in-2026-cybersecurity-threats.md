## What are the types of Rogue AI?
**Rogue AI can be categorized into three main types: compromised AI, manipulated AI, and autonomous AI.** These categories are based on the level of human involvement in the AI system's decision-making process. Compromised AI refers to systems that have been hacked or infected with malware, while manipulated AI involves systems that have been intentionally designed to carry out malicious activities. Autonomous AI, on the other hand, refers to systems that can operate independently without human intervention, posing a significant risk if their goals are not aligned with human values.
