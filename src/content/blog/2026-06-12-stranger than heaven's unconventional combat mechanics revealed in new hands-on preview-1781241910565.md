As the player progresses through the game, they'll encounter different combat scenarios that challenge their understanding of the game's world and its inhabitants. The combat is not just about winning or losing – it's about understanding the game's underlying themes and mechanics.

In many games, conflict is seen as something that needs to be overcome, to be vanquished or defeated. But Stranger Than Heaven's combat mechanics suggest that conflict can be a more nuanced, more complex phenomenon.
