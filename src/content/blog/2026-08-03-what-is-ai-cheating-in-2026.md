# What is AI cheating in 2026
> **Quick answer:** AI cheating refers to the use of artificial intelligence tools to deceitfully complete tasks, such as writing essays or solving math problems, without putting in the actual effort or learning required. This phenomenon has gained significant attention in 2026, particularly in the education sector, where students are using AI tools to complete assignments and exams.

As a concerned educator or student, you may be experiencing the pain of wondering whether the work submitted by your peers or students is genuine or the result of AI cheating. ## What are the common types of AI cheating?
**AI cheating can take many forms, including text generation, math problem solving, and image creation**. These types of cheating can be particularly problematic in academic settings, where original work and critical thinking are essential skills. For instance, tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity can generate human-like text, making it difficult to detect whether an essay was written by a student or an AI.

## How does AI cheating affect education?
**The impact of AI cheating on education can be significant, leading to a lack of learning and critical thinking skills in students**. When students rely on AI tools to complete assignments, they miss out on the opportunity to develop essential skills, such as research, analysis, and problem-solving. This can ultimately affect their performance in the workforce, where these skills are highly valued.

## What are the consequences of AI cheating?
**The consequences of AI cheating can be severe, including failure in a course, loss of scholarships, or even expulsion from an institution**. In addition to these academic consequences, AI cheating can also have long-term effects on a person's career and reputation. For example, if an individual is found to have cheated using AI tools, it can damage their professional reputation and make it difficult to secure jobs in the future.
