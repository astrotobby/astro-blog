## How does Voice AI work in Healthcare RCM?
**Voice AI works by using natural language processing (NLP) to analyze and interpret voice commands, which are then used to automate tasks such as data entry and claim submission**. This technology can be integrated with existing electronic health records (EHRs) and practice management systems (PMS) to streamline revenue cycle management processes. For instance, voice AI can be used to automate patient demographics updates, reducing the need for manual data entry.

## What are the different types of Voice AI technologies used in Healthcare RCM?
**There are several types of voice AI technologies used in healthcare RCM, including speech recognition, text-to-speech, and voice assistants**. These technologies can be used to automate tasks such as patient engagement, appointment scheduling, and payment processing. The following comparison table highlights the key features of each technology:
| Technology | Description | Benefits |

| Text-to-Speech | Converts text into spoken words | Enhanced patient engagement, improved accessibility |
| Voice Assistants | Uses voice commands to automate tasks | Increased productivity, reduced manual labor |
