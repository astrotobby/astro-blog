**Always On AI provides numerous benefits, including improved efficiency, enhanced decision-making, and increased competitiveness**. ## How does Always On AI work?
**Always On AI operates by utilizing advanced machine learning algorithms and natural language processing techniques**. These algorithms enable the system to learn from vast amounts of data, identify patterns, and make predictions or decisions in real-time. ## What are the key components of Always On AI?
**The key components of Always On AI include data ingestion, processing, and analytics, as well as machine learning and decision-making modules**. These components work together to provide a seamless and continuous flow of data, enabling the system to make informed decisions. The following table compares the key components of Always On AI with traditional AI systems:

| Component | Always On AI | Traditional AI |

| Decision-Making | Real-time decision-making | Batch decision-making |
